Sources

Selected works on which this atlas draws, organized by thinker. The list is not complete; it covers the works referenced by name across the concept pages, thinker pages, disputes, and readings. Bibliographic information is provided as factual reference.

Classical sources (in modern critical editions / translations)

  • — The Analects of Confucius. Numerous translations; benchmarks in English include those by D. C. Lau (Penguin), Edward Slingerland (Hackett), Roger Ames & Henry Rosemont (Ballantine).
  • — Mencius. Translations by D. C. Lau (Penguin), Bryan Van Norden (Hackett); Chinese critical text widely available.
  • — Daodejing (Laozi). Many translations; D. C. Lau (Penguin), Philip Ivanhoe (Hackett), Robert Henricks (Ballantine, with Mawangdui texts).
  • — Zhuangzi. Burton Watson (Columbia), A. C. Graham (Hackett — Chuang-Tzŭ: The Inner Chapters, 1981/2001), Brook Ziporyn (Hackett, 2009).
  • — Plato — Republic, Phaedo, Sophist, Timaeus. Critical text in Burnet's Oxford edition; standard English translations in Plato: Complete Works, ed. John M. Cooper (Hackett, 1997).
  • — Aristotle — Metaphysics, Nicomachean Ethics, Politics, De Anima. The Revised Oxford Translation, ed. Jonathan Barnes (Princeton, 1984), is the standard reference.

Kyoto School and Japanese sources

  • 1911 Nishida Kitarō, An Inquiry into the Good. Trans. Masao Abe and Christopher Ives. Yale University Press, 1992.
  • 1926+ Nishida Kitarō, Place & Dialectic: Two Essays by Nishida Kitarō. Trans. John W. M. Krummel and Shigenori Nagatomo. Oxford University Press, 2011. Includes "The Logic of Place" essays in which the basho-doctrine is articulated.
  • 1935 Watsuji Tetsurō, Climate and Culture (Fūdo). Trans. Geoffrey Bownas. Hokuseido, 1961.
  • 1937–49 Watsuji Tetsurō, Rinrigaku: Ethics in Japan. Trans. Yamamoto Seisaku and Robert Carter. SUNY Press, 1996. The principal source for aidagara.

  • 1981 Li Zehou, The Path of Beauty. Trans. Gong Lizeng. Oxford University Press, 1994.
  • 1985 Tu Weiming, Confucian Thought: Selfhood as Creative Transformation. SUNY Press.

  • 2011 Zhao Tingyang, All Under Heaven: The Tianxia System for a Possible World Order. Trans. Joseph Harroff. University of California Press, 2021.

Note on the corpus

Comparative philosophy of Asian and Western thought is a large and growing field. The above is an entry point. Readers wishing to extend the bibliography will find substantial review essays in the journal Philosophy East and West (University of Hawai'i Press), in Dao: A Journal of Comparative Philosophy (Springer), and in the multi-volume Routledge Encyclopedia of Chinese Philosophy and Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entries on Confucius, Mencius, the Daodejing, Mou Zongsan, Watsuji, and the figures profiled here.

Bibliographic information provided as factual reference. All glosses on this site are original notes.
