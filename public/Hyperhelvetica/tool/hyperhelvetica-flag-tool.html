Hyperhelvetica nested

Spirals of white crosses wind toward the boundary outside; spirals of red crosses wind toward the silhouette of the cross inside. The same storm at two scales, each with its own winding and its own decay. Every control writes itself into the address bar, so the link you copy is the flag you are looking at.

After the Hyperamerica flag: pledged to the flag, and to the non-Euclidean geometry for which it stands. Outside, arms accumulate at the inscribed circle. Inside, they accumulate at a boundary of your choosing (the cross silhouette, a circle, square, diamond, or four-petalled rose) and the cross is the window through which the flow is seen.
This is the standalone build: one file, no dependencies, every parameter unlocked. Open it in a text editor and change whatever you like.
